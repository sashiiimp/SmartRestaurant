# SmartRestaurant usability test: participant information and consent

**What is this?** Team ForeSight (CSE5015 Computing Project) has built SmartRestaurant, a system that helps a
fast-food restaurant decide how much food to prepare and what to order. We would like to see how easy it is to use
for someone who has never seen it.

**What will you do?** You will play one restaurant role and do four or five short tasks on a laptop or phone while
saying aloud what you are thinking. After each task you answer one question, and at the end a short questionnaire.
It takes about 20 to 25 minutes.

**We are testing the system, not you.** There are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that is exactly what we
need to know. All restaurant data in the system is made up.

**Your data.** We record only an anonymous number (for example P3), the role you played, the device, the time each
task took, your answers and our notes. We do not record your name with the results, and we do not make audio or video
recordings unless you agree below. Anonymous results may appear in our project report. You can stop at any time
without giving a reason.

# Task cards: Food Production Manager (kitchen)

*You are the manager of the kitchen. It is the start of a normal working day. The system is already open and you are
logged in.*

**Task K1 ★** How many **Chicken Burgers** should the kitchen prepare today, according to the system? Say the number
and, in your own words, where it comes from.

**Task K2** The weather looks bad and you expect fewer customers for **Veggie Burgers** today. Change today's Veggie
Burger quantity to **24** and give a reason.

**Task K3 ★** The kitchen has just cooked **24 Chicken Burgers**. Record this in the system.

**Task K4** Which **ingredients** does the kitchen **not have enough of** for today's plan, and by how much?

**Task K5 ★** At closing time, **3 portions of Fried Chicken (2 pc)** were thrown away because too much was made.
Record this.

**Task K6** You have finished cooking the **Chicken Burgers** for today. Tell the system that their preparation is
**done**.

**Task K7** **Two Chicken Burgers** were given to the staff as their meal today. Record this, and then say how many
Chicken Burgers are **not accounted for** today (if the page can show it yet).

# Task cards: Inventory and Procurement Manager

*You manage the stock room and buy ingredients from suppliers. The system is already open and you are logged in.*

**Task I1 ★** Which ingredients should be **ordered now**, how much of each, and from which supplier?

**Task I2** Create the **purchase orders** for those ingredients.

**Task I3 ★** A delivery of **80 kg of chicken** has just arrived. It **expires in 7 days**. Record it in the system.

**Task I4 ★** One batch of chicken has **already expired**. Find it and record that it was **thrown away**.

**Task I5** Is there still an **alert** saying that chicken will **run short today**?

# Task cards: System Administrator

*You run the system for the restaurant owner. The system is already open and you are logged in.*

**Task A1 ★** Which **forecasting model** is the system using now, and how much more accurate is it than the simple
"same day last week" estimate?

**Task A2 ★** A new kitchen staff member starts tomorrow. Create an **account** for them: username **kitchen2**, a name
of your choice, **kitchen** role.

**Task A3 ★** How **accurate** have the forecasts for **Chicken Burger** been over the **last four weeks**?

**Task A4** The owner wants a slightly larger safety margin when preparing food. Change the **preparation safety
buffer** to **12%**.

# Closing questions

Participant P____

1. What did you find **easiest**?
2. What was **most confusing** or slowest?
3. When the system recommended a quantity or an order, did you **understand why**? Would you **trust** it?
4. Is anything **missing** that you would expect?
5. If you could change **one thing**, what would it be?
6. (Kitchen) Was the difference between **prepared**, **served**, **waste** and **other use** (staff meals,
   complimentary portions) clear? Would you record staff meals every day?
7. Would you use this **every day** in your work, and on which device (phone, tablet, computer)?
8. (Restaurant staff only) What would stop your outlet from using a system like this?
